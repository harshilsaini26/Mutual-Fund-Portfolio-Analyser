      Returns

      What ₹10,000 became over one, three and five years and its whole record, from AMFI's daily prices.

      Its peers

      Where it stands among the funds that do the same job, on return, risk, falls and cost.

      Its cost

      The total expense ratio AMFI publishes each month, and how it compares with the category's.

      Falls and recoveries

      How far below its last high it stood each day, and how long it took to climb back.

      Consistency

      The return of every three-year stretch, not only the one that ends today.

      What it owns

      Asset mix, largest holdings and sectors, from the fund house's disclosure or, marked, an aggregator's page.
